# Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-22.

This policy explains how the Autonox Spares app ("Autonox", "the app") handles your personal data. It is written for users in **India**, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

## Who we are

The app is made and provided by **Aryan Lakra**, an individual developer based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh 492001, India ("we", "us"). We are the **Data Fiduciary** for the personal data described here. For any privacy question, request or complaint, write to our Grievance Officer, Aryan Lakra, at autonoxapp@gmail.com.

## The short version

- You can use Autonox **without an account**. As a guest, everything you enter stays **on your phone** and nothing about you is sent to us.
- If you **create an account** (with Apple, Google or a code sent to your email), we keep your account ID and email address so you can sign in.
- Your garage stays on your phone **unless you turn on Backup & sync**. If you do, your vehicles, records, photos and document files are also stored in your account on our servers **in India (Mumbai)**, so they're safe if you lose your phone and appear on your other devices.
- If you buy **Autonox Pro** while signed in, we record which subscription your account has. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or UPI details.
- **No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.** We don't sell or share your personal data, and we don't use it to profile you.
- You can **download, correct or delete** your data at any time in **Account ▸ Privacy & data**.

## What we collect, and why

### 1. If you use Autonox as a guest

We collect **nothing**. Your vehicles, records, photos, document files, part requests, profile and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We can't see them.

### 2. Your account (only if you create one)

- **Account ID**, a random identifier we create, to identify your account.
- **Email address**, from you, Apple or Google, to sign you in and send sign-in codes.
- **Name and profile-picture link**, from Google if you sign in with Google. Apple shares your name only the first time, and only if you agree. Used to show your name in the app.
- **Sign-in identifier from Apple or Google**, to recognise you when you sign in again.
- **Dates you signed up and last signed in**, for account security.

With **Sign in with Apple** you can choose "Hide My Email", in which case we only receive a private relay address. With **Sign in with Google**, the app asks only for your basic profile (openid, email, profile) and gives back its access to your Google account straight after confirming who you are.

**Purpose and basis:** to provide your account. We process this with your **consent**, which you give by creating an account. You can withdraw it by deleting your account.

### 3. Backup & sync (only if you turn it on)

When Backup & sync is on, we store a copy of:

- your vehicles (make, model, year, registration number, VIN, mileage, purchase details, status);
- your records (documents and their expiry dates, maintenance, modifications, fuel, expenses, tyres, reminders, notes, workshops and other entries you add);
- vehicle and record **photos**, and **document files** you attach (for example scans of your RC, insurance policy or PUC certificate).

Document files can contain sensitive details, such as your address or policy number. Store only what you're comfortable keeping in your account. Part-request photos are **not** synced.

**Purpose and basis:** to back up your garage and keep it the same on every device you sign in on. We process this with your **consent**, which you give when you tap **Turn on sync** after reading what will be uploaded. You can withdraw it at any time with **Turn off sync**. Turning sync off stops new uploads; the copy already in your account stays until you delete your account (see "How long we keep data").

### 4. Autonox Pro (only if you subscribe)

Apple processes your payment under Apple's own privacy policy, and we **never** receive your payment details. If you're signed in when you subscribe, we record the subscription's Apple transaction ID, which plan you have, when it renews or ends, and whether it was refunded. Apple tells us about renewals and refunds directly. Pro still works on your phone even without an account; this record only links your subscription to your account.

**Purpose and basis:** to provide the subscription you bought (a legitimate use under section 7 of the DPDP Act, and part of our agreement with you).

### 5. Technical data

When the app talks to our servers, our hosting provider automatically logs technical details such as your **IP address**, the time, and the type of request. These logs are used only to keep the service secure and working, and are kept for a short time (up to 7 days).

### 6. Things that stay on your phone only

- **Camera and photo library:** used only when you take or choose a photo. Apple's photo picker shares only the photos you pick.
- **Notifications:** reminders are scheduled by iOS on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.
- **Backup files** you create with Backup & restore: you choose where they go, and we never receive them. They aren't encrypted by the app, so keep them somewhere safe.
- **Profile details** you type in Edit profile (name, email, phone, default vehicle) and your region and unit settings.
- **Part requests:** saved on your phone and not sent to us or any supplier.

### What we don't collect

We don't ask for sensitive personal data such as passwords, bank, card or UPI details, health information or biometric data, and you shouldn't upload documents that contain them. We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers or usage analytics, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Who processes your data for us

We use a few service providers ("Data Processors") who act only on our instructions:

- **Supabase** (database, file storage and sign-in): hosts your account and synced data on Amazon Web Services in the **Mumbai, India** region.
- **Our email provider** (currently Google's Gmail service): delivers sign-in code emails, which contain your email address and a one-time code.
- **Apple** and **Google**: confirm who you are when you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
- **Apple**: processes Autonox Pro payments under its own privacy policy.

We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else. We'll disclose it only if Indian law requires us to (for example, a lawful order from a court or government agency).

### Data outside India

Your account and synced garage are stored in India. Apple, Google and our email provider may process the limited data they handle (for example your email address) outside India. The small server functions that delete accounts and check subscriptions run at Supabase's server location nearest to the request, which may be outside India; they keep nothing themselves and save results only to our database in India. These transfers are permitted under section 16 of the DPDP Act.

## How long we keep data

- **Account data:** until you delete your account.
- **Synced garage data:** until you delete it in the app or delete your account. When you delete a record or vehicle, it's removed from your devices straight away, and its copy on our server is fully erased within **90 days**. Photos and files that no record uses any more are deleted from our server at the next sync.
- **Subscription record:** until you delete your account.
- **Technical logs:** up to 7 days.
- **When you delete your account:** your account, synced garage, photos, document files and subscription record are erased from our servers immediately. Our hosting provider's backups roll off within its normal backup cycle.

## Your rights under the DPDP Act

You are a **Data Principal**. You have the right to:

- **Get a summary** of your personal data and how it's processed. Account ▸ Privacy & data ▸ Download my data gives you a complete copy as a file.
- **Correct, complete or update** it: edit any record or your profile.
- **Erase** it: delete individual records, or Account ▸ Privacy & data ▸ Delete account and all data.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it: turn off sync, sign out, or delete your account. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to: email us with their details.
- **Have your grievance addressed**: see below.

You can also email autonoxapp@gmail.com for any of these. We may need to confirm it's really you before acting on an email request.

## Security

- All connections to our servers use HTTPS encryption.
- Your account and synced data are protected by access rules on our database, so each account can reach only its own data. Photos and files are kept in private storage.
- On your phone, your sign-in session is kept in the iOS Keychain, set to stay on that phone only, and app data is protected by your phone's encryption and passcode.
- If a personal data breach happens, we'll tell affected users without delay, explaining what happened, the likely impact and what they can do, and inform the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Rules require, including a detailed report within 72 hours.

## Children

Autonox is only for people aged **18 or over**. The DPDP Act treats anyone under 18 as a child, and Autonox doesn't offer the verifiable parental consent that would be needed to process a child's data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Other network connections

The app also downloads a public file of daily petrol and diesel prices (published from Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell data) from GitHub. No personal data is sent with this request, apart from the technical details any web request carries, such as your IP address.
